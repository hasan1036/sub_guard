# SubGuard Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 14, 2026_

SubGuard ("the app") helps you keep track of your subscriptions and reminds you before you are charged. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

## The short version

**Your data stays on your phone.** SubGuard has no account system, no server and no bank connection. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information.

## Information you enter

The subscriptions you add (names, prices, billing dates, notes, cancel links) and your payment history are saved **only on your own device**, in the app's private storage. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else.

If you uninstall the app or tap **Settings → Delete all data**, this information is permanently removed.

## Notifications

SubGuard schedules reminder notifications on your device. The reminders are created and shown by your phone's own system. No information is sent over the internet to deliver them.

## Permissions the app uses

- **Notifications**: to show reminders before a payment.
- **Alarms & reminders** (exact alarms): so reminders arrive on time.
- **Run at startup**: to restore your reminders after the phone restarts.

## Links to other websites

When you tap **Cancel**, SubGuard opens the service's own website or app (for example netflix.com or claude.ai) in your browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies. SubGuard never sees your passwords or what you do on those sites.

## Ads, analytics and tracking

This version of SubGuard contains **no ads, no analytics and no tracking**.

## Children

SubGuard is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.
